Our story

Started in a hostel room, built for the classroom no one else was teaching.

VaultofCodes began in 2019 when three engineering graduates noticed the same gap in every campus they visited: students could pass exams but couldn't ship a working project. We started with a single weekend workshop on web development for 40 students in Chennai.

Today we run structured cohorts across web development, data science, DSA and cloud computing — built by engineers who still write code, for students who want to build things that work.

Our mission

To close the gap between what colleges teach and what companies actually need, by giving every student access to practical, mentor-led, project-based technology education — regardless of which campus they come from.

Our vision

A future where a student's first job offer is decided by what they've built, not just where they studied — and where "VaultofCodes trained" is a signal recruiters actively look for.

What we do

Four ways we get students from curious to hireable.

01 / courses

Structured courses

Cohort-based programs in web development, data science, DSA and cloud, built around real projects instead of isolated exercises.

02 / internships

Internships

Paid and unpaid internship placements with our partner companies, sourced and managed through our own Career Portal.

03 / workshops

Campus workshops

Weekend and hackathon-style workshops run on partner campuses, designed to get students shipping something in a single day.

04 / mentorship

1:1 mentorship

Every cohort is paired with working engineers for code reviews, mock interviews, and career guidance beyond the syllabus.

05 / community

Campus community

Campus ambassador and student community programs that keep learning going between cohorts, not just during them.

06 / careers

Career portal

A live board of jobs and internships from our hiring partners, updated continuously — no stale PDFs or expired listings.
